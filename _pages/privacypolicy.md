# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 23, 2022. Last updated: Sep 04 2026.**

Plum Village built the Plum Village Songs app as a Free app. This SERVICE is provided by Plum
Village at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and
disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the collection and use of information in
relation to this policy. The Personal Information that we collect is used for providing and
improving the Service. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as
described in this Privacy Policy.

The terms used in this Privacy Policy have the same meanings as in our Terms and Conditions,
which is accessible at Plum Village Songs unless otherwise defined in this Privacy Policy.

## Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our Service, we may require you to provide us with certain
personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained by us and
used as described in this privacy policy.

The app does use third party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Link to the privacy policy of third party service providers used by the app:

- [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)
- [Firebase Performance Monitoring](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name,
email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Everything you create while using the app — your notebooks, notes, favourites, display and
chord settings, and any song sheets (PDF) or audio files you download for offline use — is
stored only on your own device. It is not uploaded to us and we hold no copy of it.

## Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app we
collect data and information (through third party products) on your phone called Log Data. This
Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device
name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the
time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

When the app downloads song data, song sheets or audio from our servers, that request is also
recorded in our hosting provider's server logs (IP address, timestamp, the file requested, and
the app version).

## Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique
identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on
your device's internal memory.

This Service does not use these "cookies" explicitly. However, the app may use third party code
and libraries that use "cookies" to collect information and improve their services. You have the
option to either accept or refuse these cookies and know when a cookie is being sent to your
device. If you choose to refuse our cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of this
Service.

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use
commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over

guarantee its absolute security.

## Data Retention

We keep as little data as possible, and only for as long as it is needed.

**Data stored on your device.** Your notebooks, notes, favourites, app settings, and any song
sheets or audio you have downloaded remain on your device for as long as you keep the app
installed. They are deleted when you remove them in the app, clear the app's storage, or
uninstall the app. We never receive this data, so there is nothing for us to retain.

**Analytics and performance data.** Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Performance
Monitoring collect a randomly generated app-instance identifier, device model, operating system
version, app version, language, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and in-app
events such as which song was opened. Because the app has no sign-in, this data is not linked to
your name, email address, or any account. It is retained for a maximum of 14 months from the
date of collection, after which it is deleted automatically. Aggregated statistics that can no
longer be linked to any device or person (for example, the total number of times a song has been
opened) may be kept for longer.

**Server logs.** The server logs described under "Log Data" are kept for no longer than 90 days
for security and troubleshooting purposes, and are then deleted.

We do not sell or rent any of this data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## Data Deletion and Your Choices

You can delete the data held on your device yourself, at any time, without contacting us:

- To delete individual items, remove the notebook, note, favourite, or downloaded file inside
  the app.
- On **Android**, to delete everything at once: **Settings → Apps → Plum Village Songs →
  Storage → Clear storage**. This resets the app to a fresh state. Uninstalling the app also
  removes this data.
- On **iOS**, to delete everything at once: **delete (uninstall) the app**. This removes all
  data the app has stored on your device.

To have the analytics and performance data associated with your installation of the app deleted,
email us at **pvsongs@plumvillage.org** with the subject line "Data deletion request". Because
the app has no accounts, please include your device model, operating system version, and roughly
when you installed the app, so that we are able to locate the relevant records. We will confirm
receipt of your request and complete the deletion within 30 days. You do not need to give a
reason, and we will never require you to create an account in order to make a request.

You can also stop any further collection of analytics and performance data at any time by
uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

has no sign-in and does not ask any user for personal details. If you are a parent or guardian
and you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at
pvsongs@plumvillage.org so that we can take the necessary action.
